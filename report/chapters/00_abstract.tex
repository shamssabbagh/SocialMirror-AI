\noindent
\projectname{} is an agentic artificial intelligence platform designed to investigate and model the complex interplay between social media consumption, influencer marketing mechanics, social comparison processes, and psychological well-being. Modern algorithmic feeds curate idealized representations of life and consumption, frequently inducing upward social comparison, heightened anxiety, and self-discrepancy among users. This preparatory project report lays the foundational research, architectural blueprint, and experimental methodology for \projectname{}, framing it as an educational and reflective multi-agent system. The platform orchestrates autonomous agents that analyze exposure dynamics, model latent cognitive-affective states, and provide real-time, non-invasive reflective feedback to foster digital resilience.

\vspace{0.8cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} AI Agents, Multi-Agent Systems, Social Comparison Theory, Influencer Marketing, Psychological Well-Being, Affective Computing, Human-Agent Interaction.
