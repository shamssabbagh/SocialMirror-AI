# Source Quality Rubric & Journal Indexing Guide

This rubric defines the vetting process for academic sources used in the **SocialMirror-AI** project.

---

## 1. Domain-Specific Target Venues

### A. Psychology, Social Media, & Well-Being
- *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (JPSP)* — APA (Impact Factor: ~7.6)
- *Clinical Psychological Science (CPS)* — Sage / APS (Impact Factor: ~6.0)
- *Computers in Human Behavior (CHB)* — Elsevier (Impact Factor: ~9.0)
- *Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking* — Mary Ann Liebert (Impact Factor: ~4.1)
- *Journal of Adolescence* — Elsevier / Wiley
- *Social Issues and Policy Review (SIPR)* — SPSSI / Wiley

### B. Influencer Marketing & Advertising Dynamics
- *Journal of Interactive Advertising (JIA)* — American Academy of Advertising / Routledge
- *International Journal of Advertising (IJA)* — Routledge
- *Journal of Advertising Research (JAR)* — Advertising Research Foundation
- *Journal of Business Research* — Elsevier

### C. Artificial Intelligence, Multi-Agent Systems, & HCI
- *ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI)*
- *ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST)*
- *ACM Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW)*
- *Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI)*
- *Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS)*
- *Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems (AAMAS)*

## 2. Evaluation Dimensions

Each candidate paper is evaluated on a 4-point scale across three dimensions:

| Score | Methodological Rigor | Relevance to SocialMirror-AI | Source Authority |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4** | Longitudinal panel, RCT, or pre-registered meta-analysis with $N > 1000$ | Directly models social comparison, influencer exposure, or multi-agent behavior | Top-tier Q1 journal or flagship ACM/IEEE conference |
| **3** | Rigorous cross-sectional survey with validated psychometric scales ($N > 300$) | Models adjacent well-being factors or digital scrolling behaviors | Established peer-reviewed Q1/Q2 journal |
| **2** | Exploratory qualitative study, small sample ($N < 100$), or pilot experiment | General social media context without direct comparison modeling | Peer-reviewed workshop or regional conference |
| **1** | Methodological flaws, uncontrolled confounding variables, or opinion pieces | Tangential or off-topic | Non-indexed journal, preprint, or commercial report |

**Minimum Inclusion Threshold**: Candidate sources must score at least **3** in Methodological Rigor and Source Authority, and at least **3** in Relevance.
