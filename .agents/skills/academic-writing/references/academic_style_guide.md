# Academic Style Guide & Vocabulary Matrix

This guide provides vocabulary, sentence structures, and stylistic conventions required for the **SocialMirror-AI** preparatory report.

---

## 1. Verbs of Academic Attribution & Analysis
Vary attribution verbs according to the nature of the claim:

- **Empirical Demonstration**: *demonstrates, establishes, reveals, indicates, substantiates, corroborates, confirms*.
- **Theoretical Argumentation**: *posits, postulates, contends, theorizes, conceptualizes, articulates, advances*.
- **Statistical / Correlational**: *correlates with, exhibits a strong association, mediates, moderates, predicts*.
- **System Design & Implementation**: *formulates, operationalizes, instantiates, orchestrates, decouples, incorporates*.

---

## 2. Sentence Structure Patterns

### Pattern A: Synthesizing Prior Literature and System Design
> *"While classical social comparison theory \cite{festinger1954theory} posits that upward comparisons induce contrast effects and self-discrepancy, modern algorithmic feeds exacerbate this dynamic through continuous lifestyle curation \cite{fardouly2018instagram}. To address this vulnerability, \projectname{} deploys the \agentAnalyst{} to monitor exposure intensity and compute real-time comparison salience $\comparisonScore$."*

### Pattern B: Nuancing Correlational Findings
> *"Although longitudinal studies document significant positive associations between intensive screen time and affective distress \cite{twenge2018increases}, causal mechanisms remain subject to scholarly debate. Consequently, our methodological framework simulates diverse persona archetypes to systematically isolate passive browsing from active social engagement."*

## 3. Redundant & Informal Phrases to Avoid

| Informal / Fluffy Phrase | Preferred Academic Replacement |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A lot of research shows" | "Extensive empirical literature demonstrates" |
| "In today's digital world" | "Within contemporary algorithmic media ecosystems" |
| "SocialMirror-AI fixes this problem" | "SocialMirror-AI addresses this structural limitation" |
| "Influencers make people feel bad" | "Curated influencer content elicits upward appearance and lifestyle comparison" |
| "It works really well" | "Demonstrates high fidelity and stability across evaluation metrics" |
