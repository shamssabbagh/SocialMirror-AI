# Chapter Structure & Narrative Flow Guide

This document defines the structural blueprint and section-by-section requirements across the 6 core chapters of the **SocialMirror-AI** preparatory report to maintain coherent flow and achieve the targeted 40-page depth.

---

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Problem Formulation (~5 pages)
- **1.1 Background and Societal Context (~1.5 pp)**: Pervasiveness of algorithmic platforms (Instagram, TikTok); shift from social networking to algorithmic recommendation.
- **1.2 Mechanics of Digital Comparison & Influencer Culture (~1.0 pp)**: Parasocial relationships, sponsored content, commercialization of lifestyle intimacy.
- **1.3 Problem Statement & Core Research Challenges (~1.0 pp)**: Cognitive opacity of algorithmic feeds; passive consumption trap; inadequacy of rigid screen-time limits.
- **1.4 Research Questions and Guiding Hypotheses (~0.75 pp)**: RQ1 (detection), RQ2 (cognitive modeling), RQ3 (reflection agents), RQ4 (ethics).
- **1.5 Scope, Project Objectives, & Contributions (~0.5 pp)**: Scope of Assignment 2; tangible preparatory specifications.
- **1.6 Report Organization (~0.25 pp)**: Roadmap guiding the reader through subsequent chapters.

---

## Chapter 2: Literature Review & Theoretical Foundations (~8 pages)
- **2.1 Psychological Frameworks of Social Comparison (~2.0 pp)**: Festinger's SCT; upward vs downward; contrast vs assimilation; Self-Discrepancy Theory (Higgins).
- **2.2 Architecture of Influencer Marketing & Persuasion (~1.5 pp)**: Macro vs micro influencers; source credibility; algorithmic amplification.
- **2.3 Empirical Dimensions of Well-Being in Cyberspace (~1.5 pp)**: Ryff's Scales; PANAS; RSES; passive vs active consumption (Verduyn et al.).
- **2.4 Autonomous Agents & Behavioral Simulation in AI (~1.5 pp)**: MAS fundamentals; Generative Agents (Park et al.); LLM cognitive architectures.
- **2.5 Comparative Analysis & Research Gaps (~1.5 pp)**: Comparative matrix (Apple Screen Time, Forest, One Sec vs SocialMirror-AI); theoretical synthesis.

---

## Chapter 3: System Architecture & Agent Design (~10 pages)
- **3.1 System Philosophy & Conceptual Blueprint (~1.5 pp)**: Non-judgmental reflection; user agency; cognitive transparency; privacy-by-design.
- **3.2 High-Level Architectural Topology (~2.0 pp)**: Three-tier architecture (Data, Agent Society, Reflection Interface); message bus.
- **3.3 Collaborative Multi-Agent Society (~3.0 pp)**: ObserverAgent, AnalystAgent, ReflectorAgent, InterventionAgent specifications.
- **3.4 Multimodal Content Ingestion & Feature Extraction (~1.5 pp)**: Caption NLP, imagery heuristics, metadata extraction.
- **3.5 Mathematical Modeling of Cognitive & Affective States (~1.0 pp)**: State vector $\mathbf{w}_t$; comparison salience $\mathcal{S}_{\text{comp}}$; exposure index $\mathcal{E}_{\text{inf}}$.
- **3.6 Human-Agent Interaction & Explainability Interfaces (~1.0 pp)**: Cognitive mirror dashboard; XAI principles; reflective dialogue.

---

## Chapter 4: Methodological Framework & Simulation Design (~7 pages)
- **4.1 Research Paradigm & Experimental Simulation (~1.0 pp)**: In silico agentic simulation approach; synthetic evaluation sandboxes.
- **4.2 Synthetic Persona Archetypes (~1.5 pp)**: Parameterized agents (vulnerable adolescent, casual consumer, resilient user).
- **4.3 Simulation Environments & Feed Generation Protocols (~1.5 pp)**: Algorithmic stream generation; exposure schedules; feedback loops.
- **4.4 Evaluation Metrics & Validation Benchmarks (~1.5 pp)**: Quantitative (correlation to RSES/PANAS, latency, cost) and qualitative (empathy, non-intrusiveness).
- **4.5 Ethical Governance, Privacy Safeguards, & Bias (~1.5 pp)**: Local embeddings; non-diagnostic disclaimers; IRB compliance.
