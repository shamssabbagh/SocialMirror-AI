# Evidence Integration Rules & Synthesis Guidelines

This reference details the mechanics of converting raw empirical findings from Research Agent Evidence Dossiers into fluent, cohesive academic paragraphs.

---

## 1. Synthesis vs. Summarization
- **Summarization (Avoid)**: Stacking isolated summaries of papers sequentially:
  > *Paper A found X. Paper B found Y. Paper C found Z.*
- **Synthesis (Required)**: Organizing the narrative by theoretical theme, contrasting and corroborating findings:
  > *Empirical investigations of digital comparison reveal distinct affective consequences depending on target prestige and perceived attainability. Whereas general upward social comparison is linked with diminished state self-esteem \cite{festinger1954theory, appels2020social}, appearance-focused comparisons to celebrity and fitness models precipitate more acute body surveillance and negative self-evaluation than comparisons to peer acquaintances \cite{fardouly2018instagram}. This dichotomy suggests that algorithmic recommendation systems, by prioritizing hyper-idealized commercial content \cite{lou2019influencer}, disproportionately trigger acute contrast effects among vulnerable users.*

---

## 2. Incorporating Methodological Nuance
When citing empirical research, weave in methodological context to enhance academic depth:
- **Sample Specificity**: Note whether findings hold across general populations or are skewed toward specific demographics (e.g., college-aged women, adolescent cohorts).
- **Measurement Precision**: Reference the specific validated inventories used (e.g., *Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale (RSES)*, *Positive and Negative Affect Schedule (PANAS)*).
- **Statistical Modifiers**: Accurately reflect effect sizes using appropriate descriptors: *modest association*, *robust predictor*, *significant indirect mediational pathway*.

---

## 3. Explicit System Translation
Every major empirical paragraph should conclude with or transition into an architectural translation for **SocialMirror-AI**:
- *Why does this empirical reality matter for our multi-agent design?*
- *Which agent acts on this finding?*
- *What equation or heuristic incorporates this dynamic?*
